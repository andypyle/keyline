Add `DatePicker`, composed from `DatePickerContent` and either `DatePickerTrigger` or `DatePickerInput`, for picking a single date from a calendar popover. Dates are ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` strings, the same format as a native date input's value, so they carry no time zone and submit with a form as is. It follows the WAI-ARIA date picker dialog pattern: opening moves focus to the selected date (or today) in a grid of days. Arrow keys move by day and week, Home and End move to the start and end of the week, PageUp and PageDown move by month, and adding Shift moves by year. Enter, Space or a click picks a date. Escape and clicks outside close the calendar, returning focus to the trigger, and Escape inside a `Modal` closes only the calendar. `value`, `defaultValue` and `onValueChange` (and `open`, `defaultOpen` and `onOpenChange`) support controlled and uncontrolled use. `min` and `max` bound the range, and `isDateUnavailable` marks dates that can't be picked. `locale` sets the month and day names, the trigger's date format and the first day of the week, and `weekStartsOn` overrides the first day. `name` submits the date with a form, `required` blocks submission until a date is picked, and an uncontrolled DatePicker goes back to its `defaultValue` when its form resets. The trigger looks like a `Select`'s, with the same `size`, `radius`, `aria-invalid` and disabled states, and takes `placeholder` and `formatOptions`. It works inside a `Field`.

`DatePickerInput` takes the place of the trigger so the date can be typed as well as picked. It shows the date in the locale's numeric form (03/14/2026 in en-US, 14.03.2026 in de-DE), with that form spelled out as its placeholder, and reads dates back with any separators, as ISO dates or with two-digit years. A typed date takes effect when the field loses focus or on Enter, and Enter still submits a surrounding form with it. Text that isn't a date, or a date outside `min` and `max` or ruled out by `isDateUnavailable`, clears the value, calling `onValueChange` with `null`, and fails form validation with `invalidMessage` until it's fixed, like a native date input. A button at the field's end, in the tab order, opens the calendar, as does Alt+ArrowDown.
