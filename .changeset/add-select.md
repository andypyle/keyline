Add `Select`, composed from `SelectTrigger`, `SelectContent` and `SelectItem`, with `SelectGroup`, `SelectLabel` and `SelectSeparator` to organise long lists. A group is named by its label, and keyboard navigation and typeahead pass over labels and separators. It follows the WAI-ARIA select-only combobox pattern: focus stays on the trigger while the highlighted option is announced through `aria-activedescendant`, and arrow keys, Home, End, PageUp, PageDown and typeahead move through the options, skipping disabled ones. Enter, Space or a click picks an option; Escape, Tab and clicks outside close the list without changing the value, and Escape inside a `Modal` closes only the list. `value`, `defaultValue` and `onValueChange` (and `open`, `defaultOpen` and `onOpenChange`) support controlled and uncontrolled use. `name` submits the value with a form, and `required` blocks submission until something is picked. The trigger looks like an `Input`, with the same `size`, `radius`, `aria-invalid` and disabled states, and the list opens like a `DropdownMenu`'s, showing a check beside the selected option. `SelectItem` takes an `icon`, shown before its text and in the trigger while it's selected, and `textValue` for options whose children aren't plain text.
