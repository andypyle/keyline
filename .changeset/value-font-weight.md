Text controls show their values at medium weight (500), so what's been entered stands apart from help text, while placeholders stay at normal weight (400) and labels at semibold (600). This covers `Input`, `Textarea`, `SelectTrigger`, `ComboboxInput`, `DatePickerTrigger`, `DatePickerInput`, `DateRangePickerTrigger` and `DateRangePickerInput`.
