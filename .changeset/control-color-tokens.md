Add `control`, `control-hover` and `on-control` color tokens for checked `Checkbox`, `Radio` and `Switch` fills and their check marks and dots. They default to mist (`mist-500`, `mist-300`, and `on-fill` for the marks), so sage stays for primary actions and links. Set `--kl-color-control`, `--kl-color-control-hover` and `--kl-color-on-control` to restyle every checked control at once, for example back to `var(--kl-color-accent)`.
