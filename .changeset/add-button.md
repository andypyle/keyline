Add `Button` with `primary`, `secondary`, `neutral`, `inverse`, `danger` and `ghost` variants, `sm`, `md`, `lg` and `icon` sizes, and a `radius` prop (`full` by default, or `sm`–`xl` to match a containing surface). `startIcon` and `endIcon` place icons beside the label, and `iconAnimation` (`dash`, `nudge`, `hop`, `spin` or `close`) plays a one-shot accent on hover and keyboard focus, using the new `animate-icon-*` theme animations.
