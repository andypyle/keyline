Add `DateRangePicker`, composed from `DateRangePickerContent` and either `DateRangePickerTrigger` or `DateRangePickerInput`, for picking a range of dates. Its value is a `DateRange`, `{ start, end }` as ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` strings, and `startName` and `endName` submit the two ends with a form. In the calendar, the first date picked starts the range and the second ends it, in either order. Until the second date is picked, a band previews the range out to the day under the pointer or keyboard focus, and Escape or a click outside drops a half-picked range. Every day of a picked range is marked selected for screen readers. `isDateUnavailable` rules dates out, and a range can't take them in: once the start is picked, the days past the nearest unavailable dates are disabled and the preview stops short of them, and a typed end past them is invalid. `allowUnavailableInRange` lets a range run across unavailable dates, which then only can't be its ends, as for weekends in a range of working days. The calendar's keyboard, `min`, `max`, `locale`, `weekStartsOn`, `required`, `disabled`, form reset and `Modal` behavior match `DatePicker`'s. `DateRangePickerTrigger` writes the range in the locale, sharing what its ends have in common ("Mar 14 – 20, 2026"). `DateRangePickerInput` is a group of start and end fields in one box that reads dates as `DatePickerInput` does. The range takes effect once both fields hold dates, and an end before the start fails validation.
